# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Assume good intent and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, including sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information, such as personal contact details or real user log data, without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

To report a violation, use [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jwielinga/som-otel/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Do not disclose security vulnerabilities in public issues; see the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
